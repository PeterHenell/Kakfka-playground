Not consumed (lag) per consumer group

Messages · last 10 minutes
    Offsets per consumer group and partition
    Consumer groupPartitionCommitted offsetEnd offsetNot consumed

    Produced is the sum of the topic's end offsets: the offset each partition will give its next message. Consumed is the sum of a consumer group's committed offsets, and not consumed (the group's lag) is the difference.

    Pausing writes to the compacted playground-control topic. The producer stops sending; a consumer calls Kafka's consumer.pause(), so it stays in its group (no rebalance) and its lag grows until it's resumed. The protobuf consumer only commits when it writes a file (every minute), so its lag rises and drops in steps.
